Egg Empire Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Egg Empire (“the Game”) handles information when you play. It is intended for the current version of Egg Empire, which is provided through a web-based game experience.

Information We Collect

The Game is not designed to ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other directly identifying information. Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device or browser.

The service used to host or display the Game may automatically process limited technical information, such as device and browser details, IP address, crash or diagnostic information, and server logs, to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. The handling of this information is also subject to the hosting provider’s policies.

How Information Is Used

Information processed in connection with the Game may be used to run the Game, remember local gameplay settings or progress, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

Egg Empire is delivered using third-party hosting or web technology. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services and may have their own privacy policies and terms. This policy does not cover third-party websites or services that we do not control.

Data Sharing

We do not intentionally collect personal information for sale or share it for third-party advertising. Technical information may be processed by service providers that host, deliver, protect, or troubleshoot the Game, or disclosed when required by law.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the Game, contact us using the contact method provided on the Game’s App Store page so we can review the concern.

Data Retention and Security

Gameplay information stored locally remains on the device or browser until it is deleted by the player or removed by device/browser settings. Technical logs held by third-party providers are retained according to their own policies. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You can clear locally stored Game data through your device or browser settings, though doing so may erase saved progress. You can stop using the Game at any time.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the Game’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at the same public URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information listed on the Egg Empire App Store page.

Important: This policy assumes Egg Empire does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account sign-in, or a separate cloud-save service. If any of those features are added or are already enabled, update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before release.
